Hat: **supervisor**. Adopt the `phd-supervisor` persona (`.claude/agents/phd-supervisor.md`). Run this in the main conversation.

1. Read `synthesis/research-question.md`, `reading-list.md` and the paper notes.
2. If there's no current question yet, don't propose one. Ask:
   - Which 2–3 papers so far made them most curious, and what exactly about them?
   - What's a problem in applied AI × distributed systems they've seen first-hand at work?
   - Where do those two overlap?
3. If there is a question, test it against each of these, one at a time:
   - **Specific**: could two people agree on whether it's been answered?
   - **Grounded**: which papers show it's open? (Cite bibkeys from `papers/`.)
   - **Feasible**: could a micro-version be tested in Days 15–28 at 1.5 h/day?
   - **Interesting**: who would care about the answer, and why?
   - **Not already answered**: suggest a search they should run to check.
4. State the Day 5 deadline status.
5. Remind them to log any change in the revision table with the *reason* it changed, in their own words.
